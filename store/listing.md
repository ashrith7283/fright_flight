## Full description
Tap. Fly. Don't crash. Fright Flight is a one-tap endless arcade flyer where a plucky little plane weaves through drifting clouds, and every cloud group you pass is a point.

HOW IT PLAYS
- One finger, one tap: tap anywhere to lift the plane and let gravity do the rest.
- Fly over, under or between the clouds. There is always a way through, but the sky gets faster and tighter the longer you last.
- Some clouds start to drift, so stay sharp.
- Touch a cloud or the ground and it's game over. Tap to go again, right away.

CHASE THE MEDALS
- Earn Bronze, Silver, Gold and Platinum medals for your best runs.
- Beat your high score and watch the "close call" callouts when you scrape past a cloud.
- See how many runs you've flown.

MADE TO FEEL GOOD
- Day turns to sunset and dusk as your score climbs.
- Punchy sound effects, a cheerful soundtrack and light vibration on every flap.
- Toggle sound and vibration any time from the menu or pause screen.
- Pause automatically when you switch apps or take a call.

NO FUSS
- Free to play, with no ads.
- Works offline.
- Collects no personal data.
- Built for phones in landscape.

Ready for take-off?
